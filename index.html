Estimate settings & how it works

This version uses your previous paycheck to estimate combined taxes. Previous gross: $755.16. Previous post-tax earnings before benefits: $640.13. Estimated tax rate = ($755.16 − $640.13) ÷ $755.16, about 15.23%. This is a historical estimate, not a calculation using federal or state withholding tables. Your rate can change with earnings and payroll settings.

Overtime defaults to 1.5 × $26.75 = $40.125 per hour. Keep full precision until earnings are totaled. Enter worked hours only; this simple version does not separately calculate paid leave, holidays, bonuses, or shift premiums.

Benefits are subtracted once after estimating taxes on gross pay. The pretax/post-tax split is unconfirmed, so this is an approximation. Zero hours produces a $0 estimate; actual payroll may carry benefits deductions forward.

Settings from your screenshots: federal Head of Household, $2,200 annual W-4 credit, no additional federal withholding; Delaware Single with 1 allowance. These are reference information only in this historical-rate version. The $2,200 is not a weekly payment.

Nothing is sent to a server. Entries stay in this page and reset when it reloads. No login, connection to payroll, analytics, or automatic financial-data storage.
